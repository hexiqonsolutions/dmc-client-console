What’s holding the current site back

  • Beautiful story — but no clear “Order now”
  • Every order still starts with a call or Instagram DM
  • No simple form for custom cakes (size, date, theme)
  • Great photos, not yet an orderable menu
  • Delivery areas & lead time are easy to miss
  • On phone, there’s no one-flow path to finish an order

What this preview proves

  • Her logo, moss green, and real cake photos — still unmistakably Cela
  • Menu orders for bento, brownies, bombolonis & more
  • Custom cake planner: occasion, date, size, inspiration
  • WhatsApp confirmation so Anisha stays in control
  • Delivery clear: Mumbai, Navi Mumbai & Thane
  • Reviews + her baker story — trust stays personal

How to present (5 minutes)

  • 1 min — open the live site: “Where would a guest place an order?”
  • 2 min — open the preview, add brownies, send a custom cake enquiry
  • 1 min — show the WhatsApp message she’d actually receive
  • 1 min — ask: shall we rebuild this under celabakehouse.com?

What you can sell next

  • Phase 1: Full ordering + enquiry site (this look)
  • Phase 2: Order inbox (WhatsApp + Google Sheet / CRM)
  • Phase 3: Advance UPI / Razorpay for custom cakes
  • Optional: review requests after every delivery

Say this to Anisha

“Anisha, Cela already has the hard part — stunning cakes, real reviews, and a brand people remember. Right now the website tells your story, but it doesn’t take the order. I made a live preview where someone can order from your menu or plan a custom cake, then it comes straight to your WhatsApp for you to confirm. If this feels right, we rebuild it under celabakehouse.com.”
